My comps questions

Minor 1
Much of linguistic and psycholinguistic theorizing has relied on a differentiation between speakers’ competence versus their performance. Is this a valid distinction and how does it impact theories of language processing or development?

Minor 2
Discuss the acquisition of inflectional and derivational morphology in normally developing children.

Major
There is evidence suggesting that individuals who have suffered either LHD or RHD may exhibit impairments in the processing of lexically ambiguous words. Discuss the evidence in light of models of the role of the two cerebral hemispheres in lexical processing.
